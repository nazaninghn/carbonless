# Stage 4 factor audit: superseded in part

`factor_audit.csv` compared the catalog mainly with DESNZ 2024. Stage 5 reconciled it with the owner's own documents
(ATOM KABLO ISO 14064-1 calculation, uncertainty, detailed-tables and DEFRA/DESNZ 2024 files), which changes many classifications:
the 14 `atom_kablo` factors, `motorin-mobile`, `turkey-grid`, `sf6` and the refrigerant basis in particular.

Use `audit/stage5/factor_reconciliation.csv` and `audit/stage5/decision_register.md`. The Stage 4 files are kept as the record of
what was concluded without the documents; the primary-source verification (DESNZ / ETKB / IPCC / GHG Protocol values and hashes)
in `evidence_manifest.md` and `evidence/` remains valid.
